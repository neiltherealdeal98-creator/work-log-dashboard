My Plan for Cali

Protecting My Rights, Keeping Cali Stable

–
days to hearing
  • Selena (Nicole's mom) filed for full guardianship of Cali. The hearing is on the 22nd.
  • Right now I only get parenting time once a week — no legal or physical custody.
  • If I do nothing, Selena gets full guardianship and I lose my say in Cali's life.

Cali's whole life has been at her grandparents' — with her sister, grandma, grandpa, and Selena. Moving her into my place full-time right now would be a huge, disruptive change for her.

Between the three of them, they can give her more day-to-day support than I can right now. That's not me giving up — it's me putting her first.

Instead of fighting the guardianship or going for full custody right now, I'm asking the court to allow the guardianship — but with conditions attached that protect my rights and my relationship with Cali.

  • Guardianship doesn't erase my parental rights. A guardian doesn't automatically cancel my rights — I can keep visitation, contact, and a say in decisions.
  • The court can add conditions. A judge can require parenting time, give me a say in decisions, and set other specific terms. It's common.
  • It can be changed later. If my situation improves, I can ask the court to adjust or end it — based on what's best for Cali, just like a regular custody case. I'm not locked in forever.
🕐 Parenting Time
  • Increase from once a week to 2–3 days a week, with regular overnights.
  • Guaranteed holiday and birthday time on a set schedule.
  • Guaranteed makeup time when a visit gets missed or canceled.
🏥 Medical
  • Advance notice of any non-emergency appointments.
  • The right to attend appointments.
  • Full access to her medical records and providers.
  • Being consulted before any major procedure or ongoing treatment.
🎓 Schooling
  • Involved in enrollment and any school changes.
  • Listed on school records — access to report cards and teacher contact.
  • The right to attend conferences and school events.
📜 Legal & Religious
  • Input on any legal matters involving Cali.
  • A say in her religious upbringing.
📱 Communication & Access
  • Regular phone and video calls on scheduled days.
  • Notified of major events — emergencies, moves, out-of-state travel.
  • Advance notice if the guardian wants to relocate Cali.
⭐ The most important one: wording in the court order saying the guardianship is not permanent, and that I keep the right to ask for full or joint custody later — when I'm ready — without starting over from scratch.
  • Cali stays stable where she's comfortable.
  • I get real access and a real voice — not just visits.
  • I keep my rights. The door stays open for full custody later.
Next Step

Call my lawyer (already paid) and have him ask the court for these conditions before the hearing on the 22nd.
